What the tool does, step by step

  1. Areas. Built-up area = plot × FSI. Saleable area = built-up area × saleable ratio.
  2. Costs. Land and stamp duty at Q0. Approval fees and premiums spread over the quarters before construction. Construction cost escalates each year and is spent along an S-curve (slow, fast, slow). Soft costs, contingency and marketing are percentages.
  3. Sales and collections. A fixed share of the saleable area sells each quarter from launch. Buyers pay the booking amount, then in step with construction progress, then the rest at possession.
  4. Delay. A delay stretches construction and moves possession; interest is then owed to buyers under RERA Section 18.
  5. RERA account. A set share of collections goes into the separate account. Withdrawals cover only land and construction cost already spent, in proportion to completion.
  6. Tax. Charged on cumulative profit; early losses are carried forward.
  7. Rates. Hurdle rates are effective annual rates (quarterly = (1 + r)1/4 − 1). The loan rate is a nominal annual rate with interest charged monthly, as banks quote it (quarterly = (1 + r/12)3 − 1). Quarter inputs are whole numbers.
  8. Funding. Each quarter, any shortfall is met by equity first, then the loan, up to their limits. Surplus repays the loan first (never from the RERA account), is held until possession, then returned to equity.
  9. Results. NPV, IRR, payback, profit on cost, peak funding, lender ratios, break-even price and maximum land price.

Plain meanings

  • NPV (net present value): all cash in and out, valued today at your hurdle rate. Above zero beats the hurdle.
  • IRR (internal rate of return): the yearly return the cash flows earn. If the cash flow changes sign more than once there may be several IRRs; then trust NPV and MIRR.
  • MIRR (modified IRR): an IRR that assumes realistic borrowing and reinvestment rates, so it always has one answer.
  • Hurdle rate: the minimum yearly return you want before you will do the project.
  • Profit on cost: profit before tax ÷ total cost including loan interest.
  • Peak funding: the most outside money (equity + loan) in the project at one time.
  • DSCR (debt service cover ratio): cash available ÷ loan payments due. Above 1 means payments are covered.
  • Loan-to-cost: peak loan ÷ total project cost.
  • Receivables cover: (money still due from buyers + value of unsold flats) ÷ loan balance.
  • FSI (floor space index): built-up area allowed per unit of plot area.

Limits

One project, one phase. No joint-development or revenue-share deals. GST is passed through, so revenue is net of GST. Tax uses a simplified cumulative-profit rule. The RERA account balance is released at possession. The verdict thresholds are assumptions to confirm with developers.

Your data

Inputs are sent to a calculation function, used once, and not stored or logged by the tool. They are saved only in this browser so you can come back to them.
